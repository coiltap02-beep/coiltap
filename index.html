The exchange

Once it arrives: three steps, under two seconds.

01 — Tap

Hold the card to their phone

No case is too thick, no angle too odd — NFC reads through most phone cases from about a centimeter away.

02 — Open

Their browser opens your profile

No app to install. iPhone (XS or later) and most Android phones from the last six years read it natively.

03 — Save

They save your contact

One button on the page drops your number, email, and links straight into their contacts.
